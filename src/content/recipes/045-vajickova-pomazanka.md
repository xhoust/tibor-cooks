<div class="print-page">
  <h2>Ingredients</h2>
  
  <h3>The Spread Base</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>5</strong> Large eggs, hard-boiled, cooled, and peeled</li>
    <li><strong>80 g</strong> Unsalted butter, softened to room temperature</li>
    <li><strong>45 g</strong> Quality mayonnaise</li>
    <li><strong>1 tsp</strong> Dijon mustard</li>
    <li><strong>2 tbsp</strong> Fresh chives or scallion greens, finely snipped</li>
    <li><strong>4 g</strong> Fine sea salt</li>
    <li><strong>1 g</strong> Freshly cracked black pepper</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>To Serve</h3>
  <ul>
    <li>Fresh crusty sourdough, rye bread, or <a href="/recipes/011-tradicne-rozky/">Tradičné Rožky</a>.</li>
    <li>Sliced radishes and extra snipped chives.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="print-page page-two">
  <div class="print-content-block">
    <h2>Method</h2>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Prep the Eggs:</strong> Slice the peeled hard-boiled eggs in half and separate the yolks from the whites.</li>
      <li><strong>Whip the Base:</strong> Place the cooked egg yolks into a medium mixing bowl. Add the softened room-temperature butter and mayonnaise. Mash and whip vigorously with a fork or hand whisk until creamy, pale, and completely smooth.</li>
      <li><strong>Grate the Whites:</strong> Coarsely grate the egg whites on a box grater (or finely mince them with a chef's knife).</li>
      <li><strong>Combine & Season:</strong> Fold the grated egg whites, Dijon mustard, snipped chives, fine sea salt, and black pepper into the whipped yolk base. Stir gently until evenly incorporated.</li>
      <li><strong>Rest & Serve:</strong> Let the spread sit at room temperature for 10 minutes (or chill briefly) so the flavors meld. Slather generously on thick slices of fresh bread or halved rožky.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-at-bottom">
    <blockquote>
      <strong>Chef's Tip:</strong> Whipping the yolks directly with room-temperature butter and mayonnaise—without using any sour cream—creates a rich, velvety emulsion that clings perfectly to bread without turning watery. It's the exact childhood recipe that makes it effortless to finish an entire fresh loaf in one sitting.
    </blockquote>
  </div>
