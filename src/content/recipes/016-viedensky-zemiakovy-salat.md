<div class="print-page">
  <h2>Ingredients</h2>
  
  <h3>Potatoes & Aromatics</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>800 g</strong> Waxy Yukon Gold potatoes (varný typ A), unpeeled</li>
    <li><strong>1</strong> Medium red onion or <strong>2</strong> shallots, sliced into wafer-thin half-moons</li>
    <li><strong>20 g</strong> Fresh chives or flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Warm Stock Dressing</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>220 ml</strong> Rich beef bone stock or chicken stock (piping hot)</li>
    <li><strong>45 ml</strong> Apple cider vinegar or white wine vinegar</li>
    <li><strong>25 g</strong> Grainy or Dijon mustard (plnotučná/kremžská horčica)</li>
    <li><strong>40 ml</strong> Neutral sunflower oil or light olive oil</li>
    <li><strong>8 g</strong> Granulated sugar</li>
    <li><strong>6 g</strong> Fine sea salt & freshly cracked black pepper</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="print-page page-two">
  <div class="print-content-block">
    <h2>Method</h2>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Boil Potatoes:</strong> Place unpeeled potatoes in a pot of cold salted water. Bring to a boil and cook until tender when pierced with a knife (20–25 minutes). Drain and peel while still warm.</li>
      <li><strong>Slice Warm:</strong> Slice warm peeled potatoes into 4–5 mm thick rounds directly into a wide mixing bowl.</li>
      <li><strong>Prepare Hot Vinaigrette:</strong> In a small saucepan, bring beef stock, vinegar, mustard, sugar, salt, and black pepper to a simmer. Whisk until sugar dissolves.</li>
      <li><strong>Marinate Onions & Potatoes:</strong> Scatter thinly sliced red onion over warm potato slices. Pour hot stock mixture over potatoes.</li>
      <li><strong>Emulsify:</strong> Add sunflower oil. Gently fold potatoes using a spatula, swirling the bowl until potato starches release and bind with hot stock into a glossy, velvety glaze (Schlotzigkeit).</li>
      <li><strong>Rest & Garnish:</strong> Let salad rest warm for 15–20 minutes to absorb liquid. Fold in fresh chives right before serving alongside crisp pork cutlets.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-at-bottom">
    <blockquote>
      <strong>Chef's Tip:</strong> Never pour cold dressing over cold potatoes. Warm potatoes absorb the hot, seasoned stock deeply into their cores, while the agitation creates a naturally creamy emulsion without dairy.
    </blockquote>
  </div>
