<div class="print-page">
  <h2>Ingredients</h2>
  
  <h3>Salad Base</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>300 g</strong> High-quality cooked ham, cut into 2 mm julienne</li>
    <li><strong>100 g</strong> Dill pickles / gherkins (kyslé uhorky), cut into fine julienne</li>
    <li><strong>80 g</strong> Canned or blanched sweet green peas, drained thoroughly</li>
    <li><strong>60 g</strong> Yellow onion, extremely fine brunoise (rinsed in cold water and drained)</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Dressing & Seasoning</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>150 g</strong> Good-quality mayonnaise (Hellmann's/Duke's or homemade)</li>
    <li><strong>15 g</strong> Yellow mustard (or classic Slovak / Dijon plnotučná horčica)</li>
    <li><strong>10 ml</strong> Pickle brine (from the gherkin jar)</li>
    <li><strong>5 ml</strong> Worcestershire sauce</li>
    <li><strong>4 g</strong> Granulated sugar</li>
    <li><strong>To taste:</strong> Fine sea salt & freshly ground black pepper</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>To Serve</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Fresh Slovak rožky</strong> (or fresh crusty rolls / sliced bread)</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="print-page page-two">
  <div class="print-content-block">
    <h2>Method</h2>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Prep the Ham & Pickles:</strong> Slice the cooked ham into neat, uniform 2 mm julienne matchsticks. Cut the dill pickles into matching fine julienne strips.</li>
      <li><strong>Tame the Onion:</strong> Cut the yellow onion into an extremely fine brunoise. Place in a small fine-mesh sieve, rinse under cold running water to eliminate harsh raw sulfur notes, and drain thoroughly on paper towels.</li>
      <li><strong>Whisk the Dressing:</strong> In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the mayonnaise, yellow mustard, pickle brine, Worcestershire sauce, granulated sugar, fine sea salt, and freshly ground black pepper until completely smooth and emulsified.</li>
      <li><strong>Combine:</strong> Add the julienned ham, julienned pickles, rinsed onion brunoise, and drained sweet peas to the dressing. Using a flexible spatula, gently fold until every ingredient is evenly coated without crushing the tender peas.</li>
      <li><strong>Taste & Balance:</strong> Taste the salad and adjust the seasoning if needed with a touch more salt, pepper, or a few drops of pickle brine.</li>
      <li><strong>Chill & Mature:</strong> Cover tightly and refrigerate for at least 2 hours (ideally overnight) to allow the ingredients to marry and the dressing to penetrate the ham.</li>
      <li><strong>Serve:</strong> Serve cold straight from the fridge alongside fresh, crisp Slovak rožky or crusty bakery rolls.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-at-bottom">
    <blockquote>
      <strong>Chef's Tip:</strong> Precision knife cuts are key—keeping both the cooked ham and pickles at a tight 2 mm julienne ensures the dressing coats evenly and gives the salad its signature light, cohesive deli texture.
    </blockquote>
  </div>
