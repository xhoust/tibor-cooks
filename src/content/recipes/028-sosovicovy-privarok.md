<div class="print-page">
  <h2>Ingredients</h2>
  
  <h3>Lentil Base</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>300 g</strong> Brown or green lentils (hnedá / zelená šošovica), rinsed and sorted</li>
    <li><strong>800 ml</strong> Rich poultry or vegetable stock (or water)</li>
    <li><strong>3</strong> Dried bay leaves</li>
    <li><strong>4</strong> Whole allspice berries (nové korenie)</li>
    <li><strong>6 g</strong> Fine sea salt & <strong>2 g</strong> freshly cracked black pepper</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Roux, Sour Cream & Finish</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>40 g</strong> Rendered pork lard or unsalted butter</li>
    <li><strong>1</strong> Medium yellow onion, finely diced</li>
    <li><strong>3 cloves</strong> Fresh garlic, grated or crushed</li>
    <li><strong>35 g</strong> All-Purpose (AP) flour (unbleached, standard US)</li>
    <li><strong>5 g</strong> Premier sweet Hungarian/Slovak paprika</li>
    <li><strong>200 ml</strong> Full-fat sour cream (kyslá smotana, min. 20% fat)</li>
    <li><strong>100 ml</strong> Whole milk</li>
    <li><strong>25 ml–35 ml</strong> Apple cider vinegar or white vinegar (to taste)</li>
    <li><strong>15 g</strong> Granulated sugar (to balance acidity)</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Knockwurst & Toppings</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>4 links (~450 g)</strong> Boar's Head uncured knockwurst (špekáčiky substitute), halved lengthwise and scored skin-side in a tight diamond crosshatch pattern</li>
    <li><strong>15 g</strong> Rendered pork lard or butter (for searing sausages)</li>
    <li><a href="/recipes/026-tradicna-fasirka"><strong>Tradičná Fašírka:</strong></a> Crispy fried pork patties, or fried sunny-side-up eggs.</li>
    <li>Fresh crusty bread.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="print-page page-two">
  <div class="print-content-block">
    <h2>Method</h2>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Simmer Lentils:</strong> Place rinsed lentils in a pot with stock (or water), bay leaves, and allspice berries (do not add salt yet). Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, reduce to low, cover, and simmer for 20–25 minutes until lentils are tender but retain their shape.</li>
      <li><strong>Build Onion Paprika Roux:</strong> In a separate skillet, melt 40 g pork lard or butter over medium heat. Sauté diced onions for 6–8 minutes until golden. Whisk in AP flour and cook for 2 minutes. Stir in grated garlic and sweet paprika off heat for 30 seconds.</li>
      <li><strong>Thicken Stew:</strong> Ladle 250 ml of hot lentil broth into the skillet roux, whisking until smooth and thickened. Pour the mixture into the simmering lentils. Add 6 g sea salt and black pepper; simmer gently for 5 minutes.</li>
      <li><strong>Temper Sour Cream:</strong> In a bowl, whisk sour cream with whole milk. Whisk in a ladle of hot stew liquid, then pour into the simmering pot, stirring continuously.</li>
      <li><strong>Balance & Season:</strong> Take off heat. Stir in vinegar and sugar. Taste and adjust balance: the stew should be rich, tangy, and subtly sweet. Discard bay leaves and allspice.</li>
      <li><strong>Pan-Sear Knockwurst Halves:</strong> Melt 15 g lard in a skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the knockwurst halves cut-side down for 2 minutes until browned, then flip onto the crosshatched skin for 3–4 minutes until the diamond cuts fan open into crisp, charred edges.</li>
      <li><strong>Serve:</strong> Dish warm into wide bowls, topped with two crispy crosshatch knockwurst halves, a freshly fried <a href="/recipes/026-tradicna-fasirka">Tradičná Fašírka</a>, and warm sourdough slices.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-at-bottom">
    <blockquote>
      <strong>Chef's Tip:</strong> This is my girlfriend's absolute favourite Slovak dish so far. Halving the sausages before crosshatching gives you crisp, fanned-out edges while keeping the cut face flat for a deep sear in pork lard. Salting the lentils only after they soften keeps the skins tender and prevents them from remaining tough during cooking.
    </blockquote>
