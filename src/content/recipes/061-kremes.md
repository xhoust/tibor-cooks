<div class="print-page">
  <h2>Ingredients</h2>
  
  <h3>Puff Pastry Layers</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>500 g</strong> All-butter puff pastry sheets (2 standard US sheets, thawed but cold)</li>
    <li><strong>20 g</strong> Confectioners' sugar (for dusting top sheet)</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Vanilla Pastry Cream (Žĺtkový Krém)</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>1,000 ml</strong> Whole milk</li>
    <li><strong>1</strong> Whole vanilla bean, split and scraped (or 15 ml vanilla bean paste)</li>
    <li><strong>6 large</strong> Egg yolks (approx. 110 g), room temperature</li>
    <li><strong>160 g</strong> Granulated sugar</li>
    <li><strong>90 g</strong> Cornstarch</li>
    <li><strong>3 g</strong> Fine sea salt</li>
    <li><strong>60 g</strong> Unsalted butter, cold and cubed</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Whipped Cream & Finishing</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>500 ml</strong> Heavy whipping cream, chilled</li>
    <li><strong>40 g</strong> Confectioners' sugar (plus 15 g extra for final dusting)</li>
    <li><strong>4 g</strong> Unflavored powdered gelatin bloomed in 20 ml cold water (optional, for clean service cuts)</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="print-page page-two">
  <div class="print-content-block">
    <h2>Method</h2>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Bake Pastry Sheets:</strong> Preheat oven to 200 °C. Roll each puff pastry sheet on parchment paper to a uniform 30 cm × 40 cm rectangle (approx. 3 mm thick). Dock all over with a fork. Place a second sheet of parchment and a light wire rack on top to prevent excessive puffing, and bake for 18–22 minutes until deep golden brown. Remove rack, dust one sheet with 20 g confectioners' sugar, and return to the oven for 2–3 minutes until caramelized. Cool completely on racks.</li>
      <li><strong>Infuse Milk:</strong> In a heavy-bottomed saucepan, bring whole milk and scraped vanilla bean pod and seeds just to a simmer over medium heat. Remove from heat, cover, and steep for 10 minutes; discard the pod.</li>
      <li><strong>Cook Pastry Cream:</strong> Whisk egg yolks, granulated sugar, cornstarch, and fine sea salt in a large bowl until pale and smooth. Gradually temper in one-third of the warm vanilla milk while whisking constantly, then return the mixture to the saucepan. Cook over medium heat, whisking vigorously until the cream thickens and boils steadily for 90 seconds to fully cook out the starch.</li>
      <li><strong>Emulsify & Chill Cream:</strong> Remove pastry cream from heat and whisk in cold cubed butter until glossy. Transfer to a shallow tray, press plastic wrap directly onto the surface to prevent a skin, and chill completely to 4 °C.</li>
      <li><strong>Assemble Base Layer:</strong> Line a 30 cm × 40 cm straight-sided baking pan with parchment paper and place the unglazed puff pastry sheet crisp-side down in the base. Whisk the chilled vanilla pastry cream briefly until smooth and supple, then spread it evenly over the pastry base with an offset spatula.</li>
      <li><strong>Whip & Layer Cream:</strong> Whip chilled heavy cream and 40 g confectioners' sugar to stiff, stable peaks (if using gelatin for buffet service, melt bloomed gelatin gently and stream in at soft peaks). Spread the whipped cream evenly over the vanilla custard layer.</li>
      <li><strong>Top, Chill & Portion:</strong> Pre-cut the caramelized top puff pastry sheet into 12 equal squares with a serrated knife so it slices cleanly without crushing the cream, then arrange the squares gently on top. Chill overnight to hydrate and set. Dust generously with confectioners' sugar before serving.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-at-bottom">
    <blockquote>
      <strong>Chef's Tip:</strong> Pre-scoring or slicing the caramelized top pastry lid before placing it onto the whipped cream eliminates squashing the custard layers during service—use a dry, hot serrated blade wiped clean between every cut.
    </blockquote>
