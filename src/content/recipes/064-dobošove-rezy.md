<div class="print-page">
  <h2>Ingredients</h2>
  
  <h3>Six-Layer Sponge (Piškótové Pláty)</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>6 large</strong> Eggs, separated (approx. 180 g egg whites, 110 g egg yolks), room temperature</li>
    <li><strong>150 g</strong> Granulated sugar, divided</li>
    <li><strong>150 g</strong> All-Purpose (AP) flour (unbleached), sifted</li>
    <li><strong>30 g</strong> Unsalted butter, melted and cooled</li>
    <li><strong>2 g</strong> Fine sea salt</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Chocolate French Buttercream (Čokoládový Krém)</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>200 g</strong> Dark chocolate (60%–70% cacao), finely chopped</li>
    <li><strong>4 large</strong> Whole eggs (approx. 200 g), room temperature</li>
    <li><strong>180 g</strong> Granulated sugar</li>
    <li><strong>300 g</strong> Unsalted butter, softened to 18 °C–20 °C</li>
    <li><strong>15 g</strong> Unsweetened cocoa powder, sifted</li>
    <li><strong>5 ml</strong> Vanilla extract</li>
    <li><strong>2 g</strong> Fine sea salt</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Amber Caramel Glaze (Dobošová Poleva)</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>200 g</strong> Granulated sugar</li>
    <li><strong>15 g</strong> Unsalted butter</li>
    <li><strong>5 ml</strong> Fresh lemon juice (prevents crystallization)</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="print-page page-two">
  <div class="print-content-block">
    <h2>Method</h2>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Trace & Prep Sponge Layers:</strong> Preheat oven to 200 °C. Trace six 24 cm × 32 cm rectangles onto parchment paper sheets (flip parchment ink-side down onto sheet pans).</li>
      <li><strong>Whip Sponge Batter:</strong> Whisk egg whites and fine sea salt to soft peaks, then gradually stream in 100 g granulated sugar until stiff, glossy meringue forms. In a separate bowl, whisk egg yolks with the remaining 50 g sugar until pale and thick. Gently fold yolks into the meringue, fold in sifted AP flour in two additions, and finish by folding in melted cooled butter.</li>
      <li><strong>Bake Six Thin Layers:</strong> Spread approx. 95 g of batter evenly with an offset spatula inside each traced rectangle to a uniform 3 mm thickness. Bake two sheets at a time for 6–7 minutes until pale golden and springy. Immediately peel off parchment while warm and trim edges straight using a ruler and chef's knife. Cool completely on flat racks, reserving the sharpest, cleanest layer for the caramel top.</li>
      <li><strong>Cook Chocolate Buttercream:</strong> Melt dark chocolate over a bain-marie and cool to 32 °C. In a heatproof mixer bowl over simmering water, whisk whole eggs, 180 g granulated sugar, and salt continuously until the mixture reaches 72 °C and sugar is completely dissolved. Transfer to a stand mixer with the whisk attachment and whip on high speed for 8–10 minutes until tripled in volume and cooled to room temperature.</li>
      <li><strong>Emulsify Buttercream:</strong> Switch to the paddle attachment on medium speed and add softened butter 25 g at a time until silky. Beat in cooled melted chocolate, sifted cocoa powder, and vanilla extract until smooth and glossy.</li>
      <li><strong>Stack & Chill Torte:</strong> Reserve 120 g of buttercream for masking the sides and piping rosettes. Stack the five base sponge layers with an even 4 mm layer of chocolate buttercream between each. Coat the sides thinly with reserved buttercream and chill at 4 °C for 2 hours until firm.</li>
      <li><strong>Caramelize & Score Top Layer:</strong> Place the reserved sixth sponge layer on a lightly oiled parchment sheet. Heat 200 g sugar and lemon juice in a heavy stainless-steel skillet over medium heat without stirring until melted into a deep amber caramel (175 °C). Quickly stir in 15 g butter, pour over the top sponge layer, and spread rapidly with a lightly oiled offset spatula. Immediately score into 16 uniform slices using the back of a buttered chef's knife before the caramel hardens.</li>
      <li><strong>Crown, Chill & Serve:</strong> Arrange the scored caramel segments onto the chilled cake, pipe small buttercream rosettes along the border, and chill overnight so the sponge layers absorb moisture from the cream. Slice through the pre-cut caramel lines with a hot, dry serrated knife.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-at-bottom">
    <blockquote>
      <strong>Chef's Tip:</strong> Always glaze and pre-score the sixth sponge layer separately on the counter before placing it on the frosted cake—trying to slice through hardened caramel directly atop soft buttercream will crush the entire torte.
    </blockquote>
