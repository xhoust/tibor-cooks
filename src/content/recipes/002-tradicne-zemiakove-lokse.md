<div class="print-page">
  <h2>Ingredients</h2>
  
  <h3>Potato Dough (Naturally Vegan)</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>1000 g</strong> Starchy Russet potatoes, boiled whole in skins until tender, thoroughly cooled and peeled</li>
    <li><strong>250 g–300 g</strong> All-Purpose (AP) flour (unbleached, standard US)</li>
    <li><strong>10 g</strong> Fine sea salt</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Finishing Fat Options</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>80 g–100 g</strong> Rendered duck fat (traditional, savoury)</li>
    <li><em>Vegetarian Option:</em> <strong>80 g–100 g</strong> Unsalted European butter, melted or lightly browned</li>
    <li><em>Vegan Option:</em> <strong>70 ml–80 ml</strong> Neutral sunflower oil, cold-pressed rapeseed oil, or melted plant butter</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>To Serve</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Savoury:</strong> Rolled alongside roasted poultry and braised cabbage, or filled with garlic, sheep cheese, or sautéed cabbage.</li>
    <li><strong>Sweet:</strong> Brushed with melted butter, rolled with ground blue poppy seeds or walnuts, and dusted with powdered sugar.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="print-page page-two">
  <div class="print-content-block">
    <h2>Method</h2>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Prep Potatoes:</strong> Finely grate the thoroughly cooled, peeled potatoes onto a clean work surface using the fine star holes of a box grater, or press them through a potato ricer.</li>
      <li><strong>Form Dough:</strong> Scatter the fine sea salt and 250 g AP flour over the potatoes. Swiftly and gently knead by hand into a smooth, cohesive dough. Add the remaining 50 g flour only if tacky. Work briskly—resting potato dough causes salt to pull out water, making it loose and sticky.</li>
      <li><strong>Portion & Roll:</strong> Divide dough immediately into 10–12 equal portions (~100 g each) and shape into smooth balls. On a well-floured bench, roll each portion with a rolling pin into a round disk roughly 2 mm thick (~20–22 cm diameter). Dust excess raw flour off both sides before cooking.</li>
      <li><strong>Dry Griddle:</strong> Heat a heavy, dry skillet (cast iron or carbon steel) over medium-high heat with no oil whatsoever. Lay the flatbread into the dry pan and cook for 1–2 minutes until bubbling with dark roasted blisters. Flip and cook 1 minute on the reverse side. Wipe away any scorched loose flour between rounds with a dry kitchen towel.</li>
      <li><strong>Brush & Stack:</strong> Transfer the hot lokša to a warm plate and immediately brush the top surface generously with warm duck fat, melted butter (for vegetarian), or plant butter/oil (for vegan). Stack subsequent flatbreads directly on top of one another, brushing each layer so the steam and fat keep them soft and pliable.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-at-bottom">
    <blockquote>
      <strong>Chef's Tip:</strong> The base dough is naturally vegan (just potatoes, flour, and salt). Always boil the potatoes a day ahead in their skins and refrigerate unpeeled overnight; cold starch grains rice dry and fluffy without turning into wallpaper paste.
    </blockquote>
