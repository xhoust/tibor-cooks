<div class="print-page">
  <h2>Ingredients</h2>
  
  <h3>Chicken & Paprika Base</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>1,000 g</strong> Bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (4 large thighs, trimmed of excess skin)</li>
    <li><strong>30 ml</strong> Neutral high-smoke-point oil (canola or grapeseed)</li>
    <li><strong>300 g</strong> Yellow onions, finely diced</li>
    <li><strong>15 g</strong> Garlic, finely sliced</li>
    <li><strong>25 g</strong> Sweet paprika (<em>sladká paprika</em>)</li>
    <li><strong>2 g</strong> Hot paprika or cayenne pepper (optional, for subtle warmth)</li>
    <li><strong>15 g</strong> Double-concentrated tomato paste</li>
    <li><strong>350 ml</strong> Low-sodium chicken stock, warm</li>
    <li><strong>10 g</strong> Fine sea salt (plus more to taste)</li>
    <li><strong>3 g</strong> Freshly cracked black pepper</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Cream & Butter Liaison (Flourless)</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>150 ml</strong> Heavy whipping cream</li>
    <li><strong>120 g</strong> Full-fat sour cream, room temperature</li>
    <li><strong>45 g</strong> Cold unsalted butter, cubed (for mounting)</li>
    <li><strong>10 ml</strong> Fresh lemon juice</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Buttered Nockerl (Múčne Halušky)</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>350 g</strong> All-Purpose (AP) flour (unbleached)</li>
    <li><strong>2 large</strong> Whole eggs (approx. 100 g), room temperature</li>
    <li><strong>180 ml–200 ml</strong> Cold water (or half whole milk, half water for richer tender dumplings)</li>
    <li><strong>6 g</strong> Fine sea salt</li>
    <li><strong>45 g</strong> Unsalted butter, cubed</li>
    <li><strong>15 g</strong> Fresh flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped</li>
    <li><strong>To taste</strong> Coarse salt (for boiling water)</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="print-page page-two">
  <div class="print-content-block">
    <h2>Method</h2>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Sear Chicken:</strong> Pat chicken thighs dry and season evenly with sea salt and black pepper. Heat neutral oil in a wide, heavy-bottomed Dutch oven or braiser over medium-high heat. Sear skin-side down for 6–8 minutes until golden and crisp, flip for 2 minutes, then transfer to a plate and pour off excess rendered fat, leaving 30 ml in the pan.</li>
      <li><strong>Sweat Aromatics:</strong> Lower heat to medium, add finely diced onions, and sweat for 10 minutes until translucent and soft without browning. Stir in garlic and tomato paste, cooking for 1 minute until fragrant.</li>
      <li><strong>Bloom Paprika & Braise:</strong> Pull the pot completely off the burner for 30 seconds. Stir in the sweet and hot paprika until fully coated in fat without scorching. Immediately deglaze with warm chicken stock, return chicken thighs skin-side up (keeping skin slightly above the liquid), cover, and simmer gently for 25 minutes until tender (85 °C internal temperature).</li>
      <li><strong>Mix Nockerl Batter:</strong> While the chicken braises, whisk AP flour and 6 g fine sea salt in a mixing bowl. Add eggs and gradually beat in cold water with a wooden spoon just until a thick, smooth, elastic, slow-dropping batter forms—avoid overbeating to prevent tough dumplings. Rest 10 minutes.</li>
      <li><strong>Blitz & Emulsify Sauce:</strong> Transfer braised chicken to a warm platter and tent loosely. Pour heavy cream and sour cream into the braising liquid in the pot. Use an immersion blender (or transfer to a high-speed blender) to blitz the onions and braising liquid completely smooth until velvety and naturally thickened. Bring to a gentle simmer for 2–3 minutes to adjust consistency, then kill the heat and whisk in the cold cubed butter (<em>monter au beurre</em>) and lemon juice until glossy. Adjust salt to taste.</li>
      <li><strong>Boil & Butter Halušky:</strong> Bring a large pot of salted water to a gentle boil. Scrape batter through a haluškár (8–10 mm holes) directly into the water in two batches. Boil 2–3 minutes after floating, lift with a spider strainer into a warm bowl, and toss immediately with cubed unsalted butter and chopped parsley.</li>
      <li><strong>Plate & Finish:</strong> Spoon buttered halušky into warm shallow bowls, nestle a braised chicken thigh alongside, ladle generous amounts of the velvety paprika cream sauce over the dumplings, and garnish with extra parsley.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-at-bottom">
    <blockquote>
      <strong>Chef's Tip:</strong> Using 300 g of sweated onions sheared directly into heavy cream and sour cream with a blender builds a naturally full-bodied, glossy velouté without flour—finishing with cold butter off the heat locks in the emulsion and gives it restaurant-grade sheen.
    </blockquote>
