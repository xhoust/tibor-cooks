<div class="print-page">
  <h2>Ingredients</h2>
  
  <h3>The Spread Base</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>250 g</strong> Authentic sheep bryndza (at room temperature)</li>
    <li><strong>100 g</strong> Unsalted butter, softened to room temperature</li>
    <li><strong>1 tsp</strong> Sweet paprika</li>
    <li><strong>1/2 tsp</strong> Crushed caraway seeds (drvená rasca)</li>
    <li><strong>1 tsp</strong> Dijon mustard</li>
    <li><strong>2 g</strong> Fine sea salt (taste first, as bryndza is naturally salted)</li>
    <li><strong>1 g</strong> Freshly cracked black pepper</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Aromatics & Garnish</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>3</strong> Spring onions or scallions, white and green parts finely sliced</li>
    <li><strong>1 tbsp</strong> Fresh chives, finely snipped</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>To Serve</h3>
  <ul>
    <li>Fresh crusty bread, dark rye, or white <a href="/recipes/011-tradicne-rozky/">Tradičné Rožky</a>.</li>
    <li>Sliced crisp red radishes and raw green peppers on the side.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="print-page page-two">
  <div class="print-content-block">
    <h2>Method</h2>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Prep the Scallions:</strong> Slice the scallions finely, place in a small bowl with a pinch of salt, and let rest for 5 minutes to release gentle onion juices and soften the sharp bite.</li>
      <li><strong>Whip the Emulsion:</strong> In a mixing bowl, combine the softened room-temperature butter and bryndza. Whip vigorously with a fork or hand mixer until pale, light, and completely unified without butter lumps.</li>
      <li><strong>Season:</strong> Beat in the sweet paprika, crushed caraway seeds, Dijon mustard, and black pepper until the spread turns an even, warm pastel orange.</li>
      <li><strong>Fold Aromatics:</strong> Fold the salted spring onions and fresh chives gently into the whipped cheese base.</li>
      <li><strong>Rest & Serve:</strong> Allow the spread to rest at room temperature for 15 minutes to let the caraway and paprika bloom into the fat. Slather thickly onto crusty bread or sliced rožky.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-at-bottom">
    <blockquote>
      <strong>Chef's Tip:</strong> Having both the bryndza and butter at identical room temperature before whipping ensures a completely smooth, airy emulsion that won't split. Skipping sour cream prevents the spread from becoming watery and allows the pungent sheep milk terroir and sweet paprika to shine.
    </blockquote>
  </div>
