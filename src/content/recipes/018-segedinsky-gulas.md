<div class="print-page">
  <h2>Ingredients</h2>
  
  <h3>Pork & Stew Base</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>800 g</strong> Pork shoulder (bravčové pliecko) or pork neck, cut into 3 cm cubes</li>
    <li><strong>600 g</strong> Fermented sauerkraut (kyslá kapusta), drained (reserve brine) and chopped</li>
    <li><strong>50 g</strong> Rendered pork lard (bravčová masť)</li>
    <li><strong>400 g</strong> Yellow onions, finely diced</li>
    <li><strong>4 cloves</strong> Fresh garlic, grated</li>
    <li><strong>25 g</strong> Premier sweet Hungarian/Slovak paprika</li>
    <li><strong>4 g</strong> Whole caraway seeds (celá rasca)</li>
    <li><strong>3</strong> Dried bay leaves</li>
    <li><strong>4</strong> Whole allspice berries</li>
    <li><strong>700 ml</strong> Rich pork or poultry bone stock (hot)</li>
    <li><strong>8 g</strong> Fine sea salt & freshly cracked black pepper</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Cream Finish</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>200 ml</strong> Full-fat sour cream (kyslá smotana, min. 20% fat)</li>
    <li><strong>100 ml</strong> Heavy whipping cream (36% fat)</li>
    <li><strong>20 g</strong> All-Purpose (AP) flour (whisked smooth into the cream)</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>To Serve</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><a href="/recipes/003-parena-knedla"><strong>Domáca Parená Knedľa:</strong></a> Steamed yeast dumpling slices.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="print-page page-two">
  <div class="print-content-block">
    <h2>Method</h2>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Caramelize Onions:</strong> Melt pork lard in a large heavy pot over medium heat. Add diced onions, caraway seeds, and a pinch of salt. Sauté for 12–15 minutes until deep golden.</li>
      <li><strong>Sear Pork:</strong> Add grated garlic and stir for 30 seconds. Pull pot off heat, stir in sweet paprika until fragrant, then return to heat with cubed pork. Season with salt and pepper; sear for 5 minutes until meat juices release and coat in paprika oil.</li>
      <li><strong>First Braise:</strong> Add bay leaves, allspice, and hot stock. Bring to a boil, cover tightly, and simmer over low heat for 35 minutes until pork is halfway tender.</li>
      <li><strong>Add Sauerkraut:</strong> Add chopped sauerkraut into the stew. Stir well, cover, and braise for another 25–30 minutes until both pork and cabbage are fork-tender.</li>
      <li><strong>Temper Cream:</strong> Whisk AP flour into the sour cream and heavy cream until completely smooth. Stir in 2 ladles of hot stew broth to temper the cream mixture.</li>
      <li><strong>Simmer & Finish:</strong> Pour tempered cream into the simmering stew, stirring constantly. Cook over gentle heat for 5 minutes until glossy and thick. Taste and balance with reserved sauerkraut brine or salt if needed. Serve hot with warm slices of steamed knedľa.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-at-bottom">
    <blockquote>
      <strong>Chef's Tip:</strong> Tempering the sour cream with hot broth before adding it to the pot prevents curdling. Pairing full-fat sour cream with heavy cream delivers richness that rounds out the natural sharpness of fermented cabbage.
    </blockquote>
