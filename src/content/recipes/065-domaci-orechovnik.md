<div class="print-page">
  <h2>Ingredients</h2>
  
  <h3>Enriched Yeast Dough (Kysnuté Cesto)</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>500 g</strong> All-Purpose (AP) flour (unbleached)</li>
    <li><strong>220 ml</strong> Whole milk, lukewarm (35 °C)</li>
    <li><strong>7 g</strong> Instant dry yeast (1 standard packet)</li>
    <li><strong>60 g</strong> Granulated sugar</li>
    <li><strong>6 g</strong> Fine sea salt</li>
    <li><strong>2 large</strong> Egg yolks (approx. 36 g), room temperature (reserve whites for filling)</li>
    <li><strong>80 g</strong> Unsalted butter, softened to room temperature</li>
    <li><strong>5 g</strong> Finely grated lemon zest</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Stabilized Walnut Filling (Orechová Plnka)</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>350 g</strong> Raw walnuts, finely ground</li>
    <li><strong>150 g</strong> Granulated sugar</li>
    <li><strong>150 ml</strong> Whole milk, hot</li>
    <li><strong>25 g</strong> Unsalted butter</li>
    <li><strong>20 ml</strong> Dark rum (or 5 ml vanilla extract)</li>
    <li><strong>4 g</strong> Ground cinnamon</li>
    <li><strong>5 g</strong> Finely grated lemon zest</li>
    <li><strong>2 large</strong> Egg whites (approx. 60 g, reserved from dough), whipped to stiff peaks</li>
    <li><strong>1 g</strong> Fine sea salt</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Glaze & Finishing</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>1 large</strong> Whole egg beaten with <strong>15 ml</strong> whole milk</li>
    <li><strong>15 g</strong> Confectioners' sugar (for dusting cooled loaves)</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="print-page page-two">
  <div class="print-content-block">
    <h2>Method</h2>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Knead Enriched Dough:</strong> Whisk lukewarm milk, yeast, and 10 g of the granulated sugar in a stand mixer bowl and let bloom 5 minutes. Add AP flour, remaining 50 g sugar, salt, egg yolks, and lemon zest. Knead with the dough hook on low speed for 4 minutes until combined, then add softened butter in three additions and knead on medium-low for 8–10 minutes until smooth, glossy, and passing the windowpane test. Cover and bulk ferment for 60–75 minutes until doubled.</li>
      <li><strong>Scald Walnut Paste:</strong> While the dough rises, combine ground walnuts, 150 g granulated sugar, cinnamon, and lemon zest in a heatproof bowl. Bring 150 ml whole milk and 25 g butter to a boil, pour over the walnut mixture, stir in dark rum, and mix into a thick paste. Cool completely to room temperature (below 25 °C).</li>
      <li><strong>Stabilize with Whipped Egg Whites:</strong> Just before rolling the dough, whip the 2 reserved egg whites with 1 g fine sea salt to firm, glossy peaks. Gently fold the whipped egg whites into the cooled walnut paste in two additions until light and mousse-like.</li>
      <li><strong>Roll & Fill Loaves:</strong> Punch down the risen dough and divide into two equal pieces (approx. 460 g each). Roll each piece on a lightly floured surface into a 30 cm × 40 cm rectangle (approx. 4 mm thick). Spread half of the aerated walnut paste evenly across each rectangle, leaving a 15 mm bare border along the top long edge.</li>
      <li><strong>Shape & Proof:</strong> Fold the left and right short edges inward by 15 mm to lock in the ends, then roll tightly from the bottom edge upward into a neat cylinder, pinching the final seam closed. Transfer seam-side down onto a parchment-lined sheet pan, prick deeply down to the bottom at 30 mm intervals with a wooden skewer to vent steam, cover, and proof 35–40 minutes until puffy.</li>
      <li><strong>Glaze & Bake:</strong> Preheat oven to 180 °C. Brush the loaves evenly with egg wash. Bake for 30–35 minutes until deep mahogany golden brown (90 °C internal temperature), tenting loosely with foil during the final 10 minutes if browning too rapidly. Cool completely on a wire rack before dusting with confectioners' sugar and slicing.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-at-bottom">
    <blockquote>
      <strong>Chef's Tip:</strong> Folding stiffly whipped egg whites into the cooled walnut paste creates a heat-setting protein matrix that puffs and locks onto the dough layers in the oven—preventing the filling from liquefying, leaking out the seams, or leaving hollow tunnels above the swirl.
    </blockquote>
