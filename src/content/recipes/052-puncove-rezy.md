<div class="print-page">
  <h2>Ingredients</h2>
  
  <h3>Sponge Sheets (3 Sheets / 30 × 40 cm)</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>9</strong> Large eggs, separated</li>
    <li><strong>180 g</strong> Granulated sugar</li>
    <li><strong>200 g</strong> All-Purpose flour</li>
    <li><strong>30 ml</strong> Warm water</li>
    <li><strong>30 ml</strong> Sunflower oil</li>
    <li><strong>1 pinch</strong> Fine sea salt</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Punch Syrup Soak (Punčový Rozvar)</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>250 ml</strong> Water</li>
    <li><strong>200 g</strong> Granulated sugar</li>
    <li><strong>100 ml</strong> Dark rum (Tuzemák or golden rum)</li>
    <li><strong>50 ml</strong> Freshly squeezed lemon & orange juice</li>
    <li><strong>1 tsp</strong> Rum essence or punch aroma</li>
    <li><strong>2–3 drops</strong> Red food coloring</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Assembly Spreads</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>250 g</strong> Smooth tart apricot or red currant jam</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Snap Fondant Glaze</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>250 g</strong> Confectioner's powdered sugar, sifted</li>
    <li><strong>35 g</strong> Liquid glucose syrup (or light corn syrup)</li>
    <li><strong>25–30 ml</strong> Hot water or rum</li>
    <li><strong>1–2 drops</strong> Pink/red food coloring</li>
    <li><strong>1 tsp</strong> Fresh lemon juice</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="print-page page-two">
  <div class="print-content-block">
    <h2>Method</h2>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Bake Sponge Layers:</strong> Whip egg whites with salt and half the sugar until stiff peaks form. Whip egg yolks with remaining sugar, oil, and warm water until pale and voluminous. Fold the yolks and sifted flour gently into the whites. Bake 3 thin identical sponge sheets (or bake 1 thick and 1 thin) on parchment-lined trays at 180°C for 10–12 minutes until light golden. Let cool.</li>
      <li><strong>Cook Punch Syrup:</strong> In a saucepan, boil water and sugar for 3 minutes until dissolved. Remove from heat and stir in the rum, citrus juices, punch aroma, and red food coloring. Let cool slightly.</li>
      <li><strong>Assemble Base:</strong> Lay the first golden sponge sheet on a parchment-lined tray. Spread with a thin, even layer of tart jam. Place the middle sponge layer on top.</li>
      <li><strong>Soak the Core:</strong> Pour the warm punch syrup evenly across the entire middle sponge layer using a ladle or squeeze bottle until completely saturated. Spread another thin layer of tart jam over the soaked sponge, then top with the third dry sponge sheet.</li>
      <li><strong>Press & Chill:</strong> Cover with parchment, place a flat baking sheet on top, and weight down evenly with 3–4 kg of weight. Refrigerate for at least 6 hours (or overnight) so the core compresses and the syrup distributes uniformly.</li>
      <li><strong>Mix the Snap Glaze:</strong> In a bowl, vigorously whisk sifted powdered sugar, liquid glucose syrup, lemon juice, pink coloring, and hot water until completely smooth, glossy, and thick.</li>
      <li><strong>Glaze & Portion:</strong> Pour the pink glaze over the top sponge, spreading swiftly with an offset spatula. Let set for 60 minutes until the glucose syrup hardens into a firm, glossy shell. Slice into clean rectangular bars using a long chef's knife dipped in hot water and wiped dry.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-at-bottom">
    <blockquote>
      <strong>Chef's Tip:</strong> Adding liquid glucose syrup to the powdered sugar glaze provides an elastic, high-gloss shine and a clean, brittle snap when cut, preventing the fondant from cracking haphazardly or turning sticky and wet.
    </blockquote>
