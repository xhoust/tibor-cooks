<div class="print-page">
  <h2>Ingredients</h2>
  
  <h3>Base Salad Vegetables</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>1 kg</strong> Waxy Yukon Gold or red-skin potatoes (varný typ A/B), unpeeled</li>
    <li><strong>200 g</strong> Carrots (2 medium), peeled</li>
    <li><strong>150 g</strong> Parsnip or parsley root, peeled</li>
    <li><strong>100 g</strong> Celeriac (zeler), peeled</li>
    <li><strong>150 g</strong> Sweet-sour pickled gherkins (kyslé uhorky), finely diced</li>
    <li><strong>150 g</strong> Sweet green peas (canned or blanched frozen)</li>
    <li><strong>1</strong> Small red or yellow onion, very finely minced</li>
    <li><strong>4</strong> Hard-boiled large eggs, peeled and diced</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Dressing & Seasoning</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>250 g</strong> Quality whole-egg mayonnaise</li>
    <li><strong>100 g</strong> Sour cream (smotana) or full-fat Greek yogurt</li>
    <li><strong>30 g</strong> Traditional yellow mustard (plnotučná horčica)</li>
    <li><strong>50 ml–70 ml</strong> Pickled gherkin brine (uhorkový nálev)</li>
    <li><strong>10 g</strong> Powdered sugar or superfine sugar</li>
    <li><strong>8 g</strong> Fine sea salt & freshly cracked black pepper (to taste)</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="print-page page-two">
  <div class="print-content-block">
    <h2>Method</h2>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Boil Potatoes & Root Veg:</strong> Boil unpeeled potatoes in well-salted water until fork-tender (20–25 minutes). In a separate pot, boil peeled carrots, parsnip, and celeriac in salted water until tender but firm (12–15 minutes). Drain completely and cool to room temperature.</li>
      <li><strong>Blanch Onions:</strong> Place minced onion in a fine sieve and pour boiling water over it, then immediately rinse with cold water to remove harsh raw sulfur bites.</li>
      <li><strong>Dice & Grid:</strong> Peel cooled potatoes. Pass potatoes, root vegetables, and hard-boiled eggs through a wire potato salad grid (or knife-dice into neat 8 mm cubes) into a large bowl.</li>
      <li><strong>Add Pickles & Peas:</strong> Add diced pickled gherkins, blanched minced onion, and sweet peas to the bowl.</li>
      <li><strong>Emulsify Dressing:</strong> In a separate bowl, whisk together mayonnaise, sour cream, mustard, sugar, pickle brine, sea salt, and black pepper until completely smooth.</li>
      <li><strong>Fold & Chill:</strong> Pour dressing over the diced ingredients. Using a large rubber spatula, fold gently from bottom to top so potatoes remain intact. Cover tightly and refrigerate overnight to let flavors harmonize. Re-season with salt, pepper, or brine before serving.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-at-bottom">
    <blockquote>
      <strong>Chef's Tip:</strong> Blanching the minced onion eliminates harsh acidity that can cause the salad to ferment prematurely. Always dress the salad when potatoes are cool to prevent mayonnaise from breaking.
    </blockquote>
  </div>
</div>
