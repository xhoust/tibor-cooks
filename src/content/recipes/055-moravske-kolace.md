<div class="print-page">
  <h2>Ingredients</h2>
  
  <h3>Enriched Yeast Dough</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>500 g</strong> All-Purpose flour</li>
    <li><strong>220 ml</strong> Whole milk (lukewarm, ~32°C)</li>
    <li><strong>25 g</strong> Fresh yeast (or 7 g instant dry yeast)</li>
    <li><strong>80 g</strong> Granulated sugar</li>
    <li><strong>90 g</strong> Unsalted butter, melted and lukewarm</li>
    <li><strong>2</strong> Large egg yolks</li>
    <li><strong>1 tsp</strong> Vanilla extract</li>
    <li><strong>Zest of 1 lemon</strong> (finely grated)</li>
    <li><strong>5 g</strong> Fine sea salt</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Ricotta Curd Filling (Tvaroh Substitute)</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>400 g</strong> Whole-milk ricotta (strained through cheesecloth for 1 hour)</li>
    <li><strong>60 g</strong> Confectioner's powdered sugar</li>
    <li><strong>1</strong> Large egg yolk</li>
    <li><strong>1 tsp</strong> Cornstarch (or vanilla pudding powder)</li>
    <li><strong>1/2 tsp</strong> Fresh lemon zest</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Plum & Poppy Seed Layer</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>250 g</strong> Polish plum butter (Powidła śliwkowe / slivkový lekvár)</li>
    <li><em>Optional:</em> <strong>60 g</strong> Ground blue poppy seeds scalded with <strong>20 ml</strong> hot milk and <strong>15 g</strong> powdered sugar</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Buttery Crumble (Posýpka) & Finish</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>50 g</strong> All-Purpose flour</li>
    <li><strong>50 g</strong> Granulated sugar</li>
    <li><strong>35 g</strong> Cold unsalted butter, cubed</li>
    <li><strong>1</strong> Egg whisked with 1 tbsp milk (for egg wash)</li>
    <li><strong>30 g</strong> Melted unsalted butter mixed with <strong>15 ml</strong> dark rum (for post-bake brush)</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="print-page page-two">
  <div class="print-content-block">
    <h2>Method</h2>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Prep Fillings & Crumble:</strong> Press drained ricotta into a bowl and whisk with powdered sugar, egg yolk, cornstarch, and lemon zest until smooth; refrigerate. For the posýpka, rub flour, sugar, and cold cubed butter between your fingertips into sandy pea-sized clusters; keep chilled.</li>
      <li><strong>Knead Dough:</strong> Bloom yeast in lukewarm milk with 1 tbsp of the sugar for 8 minutes until foamy. In a mixer bowl, combine flour, remaining sugar, salt, egg yolks, vanilla, and lemon zest. Add yeast mixture and knead on low for 3 minutes. Slowly stream in melted butter, then knead on medium for 8–10 minutes until elastic and pulling clean from the bowl. Cover and proof at 24°C for 60 minutes until doubled.</li>
      <li><strong>Portion & Fill Curd:</strong> Punch down dough and divide into 16 equal pieces (~55 g each). Flatten each into a 7 cm disc, spoon 1 tbsp of chilled ricotta filling into the center, pinch the edges tightly shut to seal, and roll lightly into smooth buns. Arrange seam-side down on parchment-lined trays leaving 5 cm between each. Proof covered for 25–30 minutes.</li>
      <li><strong>Indent, Egg Wash & Top:</strong> Dust the base of a flat glass in flour and press firmly into the center of each proofed bun to create a deep well with a plump raised border. Brush outer borders thoroughly with egg wash. Spoon 1 generous tbsp of plum lekvar into each cavity, then scatter the buttery posýpka generously over the fruit and rims.</li>
      <li><strong>Bake & Rum Finish:</strong> Bake at 180°C (convection) or 195°C (conventional) for 16–18 minutes until borders turn deep golden brown. The instant they leave the oven, brush the hot exposed pastry rims with the melted butter-rum glaze. Transfer to a wire rack to cool.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-at-bottom">
    <blockquote>
      <strong>Chef's Tip:</strong> US whole-milk ricotta holds more free water than Central European dry block tvaroh. Straining it overnight or pressing it in cheesecloth for at least an hour is non-negotiable—otherwise, excess steam blows the sealed buns open during oven spring.
    </blockquote>
