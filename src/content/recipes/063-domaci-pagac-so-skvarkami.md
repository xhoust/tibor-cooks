<div class="print-page">
  <h2>Ingredients</h2>
  
  <h3>Yeast Dough Base</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>500 g</strong> All-Purpose (AP) flour (unbleached)</li>
    <li><strong>220 ml</strong> Whole milk, lukewarm (35 °C)</li>
    <li><strong>7 g</strong> Instant dry yeast (1 standard packet)</li>
    <li><strong>5 g</strong> Granulated sugar</li>
    <li><strong>12 g</strong> Fine sea salt</li>
    <li><strong>1 large</strong> Whole egg (approx. 50 g), room temperature</li>
    <li><strong>1 large</strong> Egg yolk (approx. 18 g), room temperature</li>
    <li><strong>30 ml</strong> Dry white wine (such as Pinot Grigio or Sauvignon Blanc)</li>
    <li><strong>30 g</strong> Full-fat sour cream, room temperature</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Crackling Lamination Paste (Select Option A or B)</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Option A (Traditional Škvarky):</strong> 300 g rendered pork cracklings (<em>škvarky</em>), finely minced, mixed with 40 g pork lard or softened unsalted butter</li>
    <li><strong>Option B (US Chicharrón Alternative):</strong> 250 g plain fried pork rinds or chicharrónes (not puffed air-baked rinds), blitzed to fine crumbs and whipped with 90 g softened pork lard or unsalted butter into a smooth paste</li>
    <li><strong>4 g</strong> Freshly cracked black pepper</li>
    <li><strong>3 g</strong> Fine sea salt (adjust depending on the salinity of the cracklings or chicharrónes)</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Egg Wash & Topping</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>1 large</strong> Whole egg beaten with <strong>15 ml</strong> whole milk</li>
    <li><strong>To taste</strong> Flaky sea salt and whole caraway seeds or sesame seeds</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="print-page page-two">
  <div class="print-content-block">
    <h2>Method</h2>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Prepare Crackling Paste:</strong> Pulse either traditional škvarky (Option A) or store-bought fried chicharrónes (Option B) in a food processor until finely ground. Mix thoroughly with the specified lard or softened butter, freshly cracked black pepper, and salt until cohesive and spreadable. Set aside at cool room temperature.</li>
      <li><strong>Mix & Knead Dough:</strong> Whisk lukewarm milk, yeast, and sugar in a stand mixer bowl and let stand 5 minutes. Add AP flour, salt, whole egg, egg yolk, white wine, and sour cream. Knead with the dough hook on medium-low speed for 7–8 minutes until smooth, supple, and elastic. Cover and bulk ferment for 45 minutes until doubled.</li>
      <li><strong>First Lamination Turn:</strong> Roll the risen dough on a lightly floured surface into a 35 cm × 45 cm rectangle (approx. 5 mm thick). Spread the crackling paste evenly across the entire surface with an offset spatula. Fold the bottom third up and the top third down like a business letter, then fold left-to-right into a compact packet. Cover and rest 20 minutes.</li>
      <li><strong>Second & Third Turns:</strong> Roll the rested dough out again to a 30 cm × 40 cm rectangle, perform a simple three-fold, cover, and rest 20 minutes. Repeat one final roll and three-fold, then chill for 15 minutes to relax the gluten.</li>
      <li><strong>Score, Cut & Proof:</strong> Roll the dough to a uniform 18 mm–20 mm thickness. Score the top surface in a tight crosshatch diamond pattern using a sharp paring knife (cutting 2 mm deep without piercing through). Stamp out rounds using a sharp 50 mm round cutter pressed straight down without twisting, re-rolling scraps gently once. Arrange on parchment-lined sheet pans and proof 25 minutes.</li>
      <li><strong>Glaze & Bake:</strong> Preheat oven to 200 °C. Brush tops with egg wash (avoiding the cut edges so layers rise evenly) and sprinkle with flaky sea salt and caraway seeds. Bake for 18–20 minutes until deeply golden brown and crisp on the exterior. Cool slightly on a wire rack before serving.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-at-bottom">
    <blockquote>
      <strong>Chef's Tip:</strong> When using US store-bought chicharrónes, pick dense, traditional kettle-fried pork cracklings (often sold at Latin markets or butcher counters) rather than airy puffed pork rinds, and increase the fat ratio to 90 g so the paste melts cleanly into flaky layers instead of drying out the dough.
    </blockquote>
