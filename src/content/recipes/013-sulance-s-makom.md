<div class="print-page">
  <h2>Ingredients</h2>
  
  <h3>Potato Dough Base</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>600 g</strong> Starchy Russet potatoes, boiled in skins the day before, peeled, and finely grated</li>
    <li><strong>180 g–220 g</strong> All-Purpose (AP) flour (unbleached, standard US)</li>
    <li><strong>50 g</strong> Coarse semolina or fine cornmeal (for structure)</li>
    <li><strong>1</strong> Large egg</li>
    <li><strong>4 g</strong> Fine sea salt</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Sweet Toppings (Choose One or Mix)</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Option A (Makovka):</strong> <strong>120 g</strong> Ground blue poppy seeds + <strong>60 g</strong> powdered sugar</li>
    <li><strong>Option B (Orechová):</strong> <strong>120 g</strong> Finely ground walnuts + <strong>50 g</strong> powdered sugar</li>
    <li><strong>Option C (Strúhanka):</strong> <strong>100 g</strong> Fine breadcrumbs toasted in <strong>30 g</strong> butter + <strong>40 g</strong> powdered sugar</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>To Finish & Serve</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>100 g</strong> Unsalted butter, melted until golden and nutty</li>
    <li><strong>4 L</strong> Water + <strong>15 g</strong> Fine sea salt (for boiling)</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="print-page page-two">
  <div class="print-content-block">
    <h2>Method</h2>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Prep Potato Mash:</strong> Ensure boiled potatoes are completely cold (overnight refrigeration preferred). Grate them on the finest setting of a box grater onto a clean work surface.</li>
      <li><strong>Form Dough:</strong> Add egg, sea salt, coarse semolina, and 180 g AP flour. Knead swiftly with your hands for 2–3 minutes just until a smooth, non-sticky dough comes together. Add remaining flour only if dough feels wet. Do not overwork.</li>
      <li><strong>Roll Šúľance:</strong> Divide dough into 4 manageable logs. Roll each log on a lightly floured board into finger-thick ropes (~1.5 cm diameter). Cut crosswise into 2 cm nuggets. Roll each nugget under your palm against the counter to form tapered cylinders.</li>
      <li><strong>Boil:</strong> Bring salted water to a rolling boil. Drop šúľance in batches and stir gently from the bottom to prevent sticking. Once they float to the top, cook for 2–3 minutes until tender.</li>
      <li><strong>Glaze & Dress:</strong> Lift dumplings out with a spider strainer directly into a wide bowl containing half the hot melted butter. Toss immediately to coat.</li>
      <li><strong>Plate:</strong> Plate warm šúľance, dust generously with your chosen topping (poppy seed, walnut, or toasted breadcrumb blend), and ladle warm foaming butter over the top.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-at-bottom">
    <blockquote>
      <strong>Chef's Tip:</strong> Boiling potatoes in their skins a day ahead allows excess moisture to evaporate. Working fast once the salt hits the grated potatoes prevents the starch from turning watery and keeps the dumplings light.
    </blockquote>
  </div>
</div>
