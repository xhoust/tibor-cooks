<div class="print-page">
  <h2>Ingredients</h2>
  
  <h3>Caramelized Sauerkraut & Bacon Base</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>500 g</strong> Naturally fermented sauerkraut (kyslá kapusta), drained and roughly chopped</li>
    <li><strong>200 g</strong> Smoked slab bacon or double-smoked pork belly (údená slanina / pancetta), diced into 8 mm lardons</li>
    <li><strong>25 g</strong> Rendered pork lard (bravčová masť)</li>
    <li><strong>1</strong> Medium yellow onion, finely diced</li>
    <li><strong>3 g</strong> Whole caraway seeds (celá rasca)</li>
    <li><strong>8 g</strong> Granulated sugar (to balance acidity)</li>
    <li><strong>2 g</strong> Freshly cracked black pepper</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Potato Halušky Base</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>600 g</strong> Starchy Russet potatoes (peeled and finely grated raw into wet pulp)</li>
    <li><strong>220 g–260 g</strong> All-Purpose (AP) flour (unbleached, standard US)</li>
    <li><strong>1</strong> Large egg</li>
    <li><strong>6 g</strong> Fine sea salt</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>To Cook & Serve</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>4 L</strong> Water + <strong>15 g</strong> Fine sea salt (for cooking halušky)</li>
    <li>Crisp rendered bacon bits and warm rendered fat for garnish</li>
    <li><a href="/recipes/001-bryndzove-halusky"><strong>Bryndzové Halušky:</strong></a> Recommended to serve alongside for a traditional half-and-half platter (pol na pol).</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="print-page page-two">
  <div class="print-content-block">
    <h2>Method</h2>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Render Bacon:</strong> In a wide, heavy skillet over medium heat, render diced smoked bacon in pork lard until golden and crisp (6–8 minutes). Lift out half of the crispy lardons with a slotted spoon and reserve for garnish; leave all liquid rendered fat in the pan.</li>
      <li><strong>Caramelize Sauerkraut:</strong> Add finely diced onion and whole caraway seeds into the hot bacon fat. Sauté for 6–8 minutes until soft and golden. Add chopped sauerkraut, sugar, and cracked black pepper. Lower heat to medium-low, cover, and braise for 20 minutes until cabbage softens and absorbs the smoky fat.</li>
      <li><strong>Prep Potato Pulp:</strong> Finely grate raw peeled potatoes on the smallest holes of a box grater (or purée in a food processor) into a large bowl. Do not strain out any potato liquid.</li>
      <li><strong>Mix Halušky Batter:</strong> Stir egg and 6 g fine sea salt directly into the grated potato pulp. Fold in All-Purpose flour with a wooden spoon just until a wet, tacky, dropping batter forms.</li>
      <li><strong>Boil Dumplings:</strong> Bring 4 L of salted water to a rapid rolling boil on the cooktop. Working in batches, press the batter through a haluškovač (dumpling sieve) directly into the water, scraping across the holes with a scraper. Stir once gently to prevent sticking.</li>
      <li><strong>Toss & Serve:</strong> Once dumplings float to the surface, boil for 90 seconds. Transfer them with a spider strainer directly into the pan of hot caramelized sauerkraut. Toss vigorously over low heat for 1 minute until every dumpling is glossy. Spoon over reserved crispy bacon lardons and serve immediately.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-at-bottom">
    <blockquote>
      <strong>Chef's Tip:</strong> Strapačky are at their absolute best when enjoyed side-by-side with a bowl of warm <a href="/recipes/001-bryndzove-halusky">Bryndzové Halušky</a>. The sharp, savory tang of the sheep cheese balances the rich, caramelized acidity of the sauerkraut and smoked pork lardons.
    </blockquote>
