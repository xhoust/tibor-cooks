<div class="print-page">
  <h2>Ingredients</h2>
  
  <h3>Peppers & Meat Filling</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>6</strong> Light green Cubanelle or Hungarian wax peppers (svetlá TV paprika)</li>
    <li><strong>500 g</strong> Minced pork shoulder (bravčové mleté pliecko, ~20% fat)</li>
    <li><strong>80 g</strong> Parboiled or medium-grain rice (rinsed and drained)</li>
    <li><strong>1</strong> Large egg</li>
    <li><strong>1</strong> Medium yellow onion, very finely minced</li>
    <li><strong>3 cloves</strong> Fresh garlic, grated</li>
    <li><strong>15 g</strong> Rendered pork lard (bravčová masť) or sunflower oil (for onions)</li>
    <li><strong>6 g</strong> Premier sweet Hungarian/Slovak paprika</li>
    <li><strong>1 g</strong> Ground black pepper</li>
    <li><strong>6 g</strong> Fine sea salt</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Tomato Gravy (Paradajková Omáčka)</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>40 g</strong> Rendered pork lard or unsalted butter</li>
    <li><strong>40 g</strong> All-Purpose (AP) flour (unbleached, standard US)</li>
    <li><strong>140 g</strong> Double-concentrated tomato paste (paradajkový pretlak)</li>
    <li><strong>700 g</strong> Tomato passata / crushed sieved tomatoes</li>
    <li><strong>500 ml</strong> Rich poultry or beef stock (hot)</li>
    <li><strong>40 g–50 g</strong> Granulated sugar (to balance acidity, to taste)</li>
    <li><strong>3</strong> Dried bay leaves</li>
    <li><strong>4</strong> Whole allspice berries (nové korenie)</li>
    <li><strong>1 pinch</strong> Ground cinnamon (optional traditional touch)</li>
    <li><strong>5 g</strong> Fine sea salt (to taste)</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>To Serve</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><a href="/recipes/003-parena-knedla"><strong>Domáca Parená Knedľa:</strong></a> Warm steamed yeast dumpling slices, or boiled baby potatoes.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="print-page page-two">
  <div class="print-content-block">
    <h2>Method</h2>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Sauté Aromatics:</strong> Melt 15 g pork lard in a skillet over medium heat. Sauté minced onions for 5–6 minutes until soft and translucent. Add grated garlic and sweet paprika, stir off heat for 30 seconds, and let cool slightly.</li>
      <li><strong>Mix Meat Filling:</strong> In a large bowl, combine minced pork, rinsed raw rice, sautéed onion-garlic mixture, raw egg, 6 g salt, and black pepper. Mix thoroughly by hand until sticky and cohesive.</li>
      <li><strong>Prep & Stuff Peppers:</strong> Cut tops off peppers and scoop out seeds and inner white ribs. Stuff each pepper loosely with meat filling (leave 1 cm headspace at top for rice expansion during poaching). Roll any leftover filling into 4–5 cm meatballs (guľky).</li>
      <li><strong>Build Stovetop Tomato Sauce:</strong> In a wide Dutch oven or deep heavy pot, melt 40 g pork lard over medium heat. Whisk in AP flour and cook for 2 minutes to form a pale golden roux (zápražka). Whisk in tomato paste and cook for 1 minute until fragrant. Gradually pour in hot stock and tomato passata, whisking vigorously to avoid lumps.</li>
      <li><strong>Season & Simmer:</strong> Add bay leaves, allspice berries, sugar, pinch of cinnamon, and salt. Bring to a gentle boil, stirring until smooth and lightly thickened.</li>
      <li><strong>Poach Stuffed Peppers:</strong> Submerge stuffed peppers and free meatballs into simmering sauce in a single layer. Lower heat to medium-low, cover with a lid, and simmer gently for 40–45 minutes until meat is fully cooked and rice is tender.</li>
      <li><strong>Adjust & Serve:</strong> Discard bay leaves and allspice. Taste sauce and adjust balance with extra sugar or salt if needed. Serve piping hot with warm slices of steamed <a href="/recipes/003-parena-knedla">Domáca Parená Knedľa</a> or boiled potatoes.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-at-bottom">
    <blockquote>
      <strong>Chef's Tip:</strong> Never pack the meat filling tightly into the peppers; the raw rice grains absorb moisture from the meat and tomato sauce as they simmer, expanding significantly. Leaving slack prevents peppers from splitting open during the stovetop poach.
    </blockquote>
