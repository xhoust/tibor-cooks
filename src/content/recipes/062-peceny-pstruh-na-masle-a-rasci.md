<div class="print-page">
  <h2>Ingredients</h2>
  
  <h3>Rainbow Trout</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>4 whole (approx. 300 g–350 g each)</strong> Fresh rainbow trout, gutted, scaled, and gills removed</li>
    <li><strong>12 g</strong> Fine sea salt</li>
    <li><strong>3 g</strong> Freshly cracked black pepper</li>
    <li><strong>40 g</strong> All-Purpose (AP) flour (unbleached, for light dusting)</li>
    <li><strong>30 ml</strong> Neutral high-smoke-point oil (canola or grapeseed)</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Caraway Brown Butter (Rascové Maslo)</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>100 g</strong> Unsalted butter, cubed</li>
    <li><strong>8 g</strong> Whole caraway seeds (<em>rasca</em>), lightly crushed in a mortar</li>
    <li><strong>2 cloves (approx. 8 g)</strong> Garlic, lightly smashed with skin on</li>
    <li><strong>20 ml</strong> Fresh lemon juice (plus 1 whole lemon, cut into wedges for service)</li>
    <li><strong>15 g</strong> Fresh flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="print-page page-two">
  <div class="print-content-block">
    <h2>Method</h2>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Score & Season Trout:</strong> Pat the whole rainbow trout completely dry inside and out with paper towels. Make three shallow diagonal scores (approx. 3 mm deep) across the thickest part of each flank to prevent curling and ensure even heat penetration. Season cavities and skin evenly with fine sea salt and black pepper.</li>
      <li><strong>Dredge Lightly:</strong> Dust the exterior of each trout lightly in AP flour, shaking off every bit of excess so only a whisper-thin coating remains to protect the delicate skin and promote crisp browning.</li>
      <li><strong>Pan-Roast Skin:</strong> Heat neutral oil in two large stainless-steel or heavy cast-iron skillets over medium-high heat until shimmering. Lay two trout in each pan facing away from you, pressing gently with a fish spatula for 10 seconds to keep the skin flat. Sear undisturbed for 4–5 minutes until the skin is golden, blistered, and releases naturally.</li>
      <li><strong>Flip & Baste with Caraway Butter:</strong> Carefully turn the trout over and lower heat to medium. Divide the cubed unsalted butter, lightly crushed caraway seeds, and smashed garlic between the pans. Once the butter foams and turns nutty hazelnut brown, tilt the pans and baste the fish continuously for 3–4 minutes until the flesh near the spine reaches 60 °C and flakes cleanly.</li>
      <li><strong>Finish & Plate:</strong> Kill the heat, squeeze fresh lemon juice into the foaming caraway brown butter, and scatter in chopped parsley. Transfer each trout to a warm platter, spoon the sizzling caraway butter pan sauce generously over the crisp skin, and serve immediately with lemon wedges.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-at-bottom">
    <blockquote>
      <strong>Chef's Tip:</strong> Lightly bruising the whole caraway seeds just before they hit the foaming butter fractures their hulls and releases aromatic oils immediately without burning—keep the butter moving so the milk solids toast evenly to hazelnut brown rather than blackening.
    </blockquote>
  </div>
