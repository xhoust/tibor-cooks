<div class="print-page">
  <h2>Ingredients</h2>
  
  <h3>The Mousse Base</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>250 g</strong> High-quality cooked deli ham, roughly diced</li>
    <li><strong>110 g</strong> Unsalted butter, softened to room temperature</li>
    <li><strong>35 g</strong> Quality mayonnaise</li>
    <li><strong>1 tsp</strong> Dijon mustard</li>
    <li><strong>1 pinch</strong> Sweet paprika (for subtle color and depth)</li>
    <li><strong>2 g</strong> Fine sea salt (adjust to taste depending on ham saltiness)</li>
    <li><strong>1 pinch</strong> Ground white or black pepper</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Garnish & To Serve</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>2–3 tbsp</strong> Fresh chives, finely snipped</li>
    <li>Warm crusty bread, toasted brioche, or fresh <a href="/blog/011-tradicne-rozky/">Tradičné Rožky</a>.</li>
    <li>Thinly sliced sweet pickled gherkins (optional).</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="print-page page-two">
  <div class="print-content-block">
    <h2>Method</h2>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Mince the Ham:</strong> Place the diced cooked ham into the bowl of a food processor. Pulse continuously until reduced to a very fine, uniform crumb.</li>
      <li><strong>Blend with Butter & Mayo:</strong> Add the softened room-temperature butter, mayonnaise, Dijon mustard, sweet paprika, white pepper, and salt into the processor with the ham.</li>
      <li><strong>Aerate into a Mousse:</strong> Run the food processor on high for 2 to 3 minutes, stopping once or twice to scrape down the sides with a spatula, until the mixture transforms into a pale pink, silky, and fully aerated mousse.</li>
      <li><strong>Chill Briefly:</strong> Transfer the mousse to a serving bowl or pipe into ramekins. Rest in the fridge for 20 minutes to lightly stabilize the butter structure.</li>
      <li><strong>Finish & Serve:</strong> Slather or pipe generously onto fresh bread or halved rožky, finishing with a dense blanket of freshly snipped chives over the top.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-at-bottom">
    <blockquote>
      <strong>Chef's Tip:</strong> For that textbook deli-style mousse texture, make sure the butter is completely softened before blending. Running the food processor long enough emulsifies the ham fibers into the fat, creating a featherlight spread that pairs perfectly with the sharp, clean onion freshness of garden chives.
    </blockquote>
  </div>
