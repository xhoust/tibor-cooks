<div class="print-page">
  <h2>Ingredients</h2>
  
  <h3>Meat & Bone Base (Choose One or Combine)</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Kurací (Chicken):</strong> <strong>1.5 kg</strong> Whole soup hen / stewing chicken or bone-in chicken backs, wings, and neck</li>
    <li><strong>Morčací (Turkey):</strong> <strong>1.5 kg</strong> Turkey wings, neck, and bone-in drumstick</li>
    <li><strong>Hovädzí (Beef):</strong> <strong>1.2 kg</strong> Beef shank (glejovka), beef short ribs, or marrow bones (špikové kosti)</li>
    <li><strong>4 L</strong> Cold water (always start from cold)</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Root Vegetables, Aromatics & Fresh Lovage</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>300 g</strong> Carrots (mrkva), peeled and halved lengthwise</li>
    <li><strong>200 g</strong> Parsnips or root parsley (petržlen), peeled and halved</li>
    <li><strong>150 g</strong> Celeriac root (zeler), peeled and cut into large chunks</li>
    <li><strong>100 g</strong> Kohlrabi (kaleráb), peeled and quartered</li>
    <li><strong>1</strong> Large yellow onion (unpeeled, halved horizontally for charred base)</li>
    <li><strong>1/2 head</strong> Fresh garlic (cut crosswise across the bulbs)</li>
    <li><strong>1</strong> Small leek (pór), dark green tops rinsed thoroughly</li>
    <li><strong>3–4 sprigs</strong> Fresh lovage leaves (ligurček / maggi bylina) or <strong>15 ml</strong> homemade vibrant lovage oil</li>
    <li><strong>1 small bunch</strong> Fresh flat-leaf parsley sprigs & celery leaves</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Whole Spices & Seasoning</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>8</strong> Whole black peppercorns (celé čierne korenie)</li>
    <li><strong>5</strong> Whole allspice berries (nové korenie)</li>
    <li><strong>2</strong> Dried bay leaves</li>
    <li><strong>1 pinch</strong> Ground nutmeg or small piece of whole mace flower (muškátový kvet)</li>
    <li><strong>15 g–18 g</strong> Fine sea salt (to taste)</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>To Serve</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><a href="/blog/035-domace-rezance-a-fliacky"><strong>Domáce Tenké Rezance:</strong></a> Handcrafted fine soup egg vermicelli, boiled separately.</li>
    <li>Thinly sliced tender cooked carrots, shredded poultry or beef, drops of lovage oil, and chopped fresh parsley.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="print-page page-two">
  <div class="print-content-block">
    <h2>Method</h2>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Char the Onion:</strong> Place the unpeeled onion halves cut-side down into a hot, dry heavy skillet over medium-high heat. Char without oil for 4–5 minutes until the cut faces are deeply blackened (this imparts an amber glow and rich depth to the broth).</li>
      <li><strong>Initial Blanch & Cold Start:</strong> Rinse meat and bones under cold water. Place into a large stockpot, cover with 4 L cold water, and bring slowly to a gentle simmer over medium heat. As it approaches a boil, skim off any grey protein foam and scum (peny) that rises to the surface with a fine mesh ladle.</li>
      <li><strong>Add Spices & Bare Simmer:</strong> Once the surface is clear, add charred onion halves, garlic head, bay leaves, peppercorns, allspice, and 12 g sea salt. Reduce heat immediately to the lowest setting. Maintain a gentle "smile" (tiahnutie)—a slow, lazy bubble breaking the surface without rolling. Simmer covered with the lid slightly ajar:
        <ul>
          <li><strong>Chicken / Turkey:</strong> Simmer gently for 2 hours.</li>
          <li><strong>Beef:</strong> Simmer gently for 3 to 4 hours until meat is tender.</li>
        </ul>
      </li>
      <li><strong>Add Root Vegetables & Lovage:</strong> Add peeled carrots, parsley roots, celeriac, kohlrabi, leek, bundled parsley greens, and fresh lovage sprigs. Continue to simmer gently for an additional 60–75 minutes until vegetables are fork-tender but hold their clean shape.</li>
      <li><strong>Strain & Clarify:</strong> Turn off heat. Gently lift out meat and vegetables with tongs and set aside to cool slightly. Pour the broth through a fine-mesh sieve lined with cheesecloth or a clean linen towel into a clean pot. Do not press down on solids to keep the broth crystal clear.</li>
      <li><strong>Final Seasoning:</strong> Skim off excess surface fat if desired. Stir in a microplane pinch of fresh nutmeg and adjust seasoning with extra sea salt.</li>
      <li><strong>Assemble & Serve:</strong> Place a portion of separately boiled <a href="/blog/035-domace-rezance-a-fliacky">Domáce Tenké Rezance</a> into warm soup bowls. Add sliced carrots, picked meat pieces, ladle over boiling hot clear broth, and finish with a drop of lovage oil and fresh chopped parsley.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-at-bottom">
    <blockquote>
      <strong>Chef's Tip:</strong> Fresh lovage (ligurček) or homemade vibrant green lovage oil is an absolute non-negotiable—its savory, celery-like aroma is unforgettable. Growing up, I actually used to dislike this dish because my maternal grandma made it every single Sunday straight from our backyard chicken coop. As I grew older and became a chef, I realized how profound that experience was: witnessing the entire cycle from the yard to the pot taught me the foundational respect for ingredients and stock-making that defines my cooking today.
    </blockquote>
