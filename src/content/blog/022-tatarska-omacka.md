<div class="print-page">
  <h2>Ingredients</h2>
  
  <h3>Tartar Sauce Base</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>220 g</strong> Quality whole-egg mayonnaise</li>
    <li><strong>25 g</strong> French shallot, very finely minced</li>
    <li><strong>45 g</strong> Sweet-sour pickled gherkins (kyslé uhorky), finely grated or finely brunoised, gently drained</li>
    <li><strong>15 g</strong> Capers in brine, drained and finely chopped</li>
    <li><strong>10 g</strong> Fresh flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped</li>
    <li><strong>25 ml–35 ml</strong> Pickled gherkin brine (uhorkový nálev)</li>
    <li><strong>5 g</strong> Traditional yellow or Dijon mustard (plnotučná horčica)</li>
    <li><strong>2 g</strong> Powdered sugar or superfine granulated sugar (optional, to balance sharpness)</li>
    <li><strong>1 g</strong> Fine sea salt & freshly cracked black pepper (to taste)</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Recommended Pairings</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><a href="/blog/017-vyprazany-syr"><strong>Vyprážaný Syr:</strong></a> Crispy fried breaded cheese.</li>
    <li><a href="/blog/021-zbojnicka-kapsa"><strong>Zbojnícka Kapsa:</strong></a> Crispy potato pancake pocket with chicken sauté.</li>
    <li><a href="/blog/014-vyprazany-bravcovy-rezen"><strong>Vyprážaný Bravčový Rezeň:</strong></a> Golden pan-fried pork cutlets.</li>
  </ul>

<div class="print-page page-two">
  <div class="print-content-block">
    <h2>Method</h2>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Prep Aromatics:</strong> Very finely mince the shallot. Finely grate or brunoise the pickled gherkins, then press lightly with a paper towel to remove excess liquid. Finely chop the capers and flat-leaf parsley.</li>
      <li><strong>Emulsify Sauce:</strong> In a medium mixing bowl, whisk together mayonnaise, mustard, and 25 ml of pickled gherkin brine until smooth and glossy.</li>
      <li><strong>Fold Aromatics:</strong> Stir in minced shallots, drained gherkins, chopped capers, and fresh parsley using a spatula until evenly distributed.</li>
      <li><strong>Season & Balance:</strong> Taste and adjust with salt, freshly cracked black pepper, a pinch of sugar, and an extra splash of pickle brine if higher acidity is desired.</li>
      <li><strong>Chill & Serve:</strong> Cover tightly and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before serving to allow shallot and caper flavors to infuse into the mayonnaise.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-at-bottom">
    <blockquote>
      <strong>Chef's Tip:</strong> Draining grated gherkins before folding keeps the sauce thick and stable rather than watery. Using straight pickle brine instead of raw vinegar provides a rounded, herbal acidity that cuts cleanly through fried dishes.
    </blockquote>
