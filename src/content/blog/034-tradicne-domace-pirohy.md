<div class="print-page">
  <h2>Ingredients</h2>
  
  <h3>Pliable Potato Dough Base</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>500 g</strong> Starchy Russet potatoes, boiled in skins the day before, peeled, and finely grated</li>
    <li><strong>200 g–230 g</strong> All-Purpose (AP) flour (unbleached, standard US)</li>
    <li><strong>1</strong> Large egg</li>
    <li><strong>4 g</strong> Fine sea salt</li>
    <li><strong>20 ml–30 ml</strong> Lukewarm water (only if needed to bring dough together)</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Savory Fillings (Zemiaková & Bryndzová)</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Potato-Onion (Zemiaková):</strong> <strong>300 g</strong> Warm mashed potatoes whipped with <strong>30 g</strong> butter, <strong>1</strong> finely minced sautéed onion, salt, and black pepper</li>
    <li><strong>Bryndza-Potato (Bryndzová):</strong> <strong>200 g</strong> Warm mashed potatoes mixed with <strong>150 g</strong> authentic Bryndza (or 50/50 mix of mild sheep feta and creamy whole-milk ricotta) and <strong>1 pinch</strong> ground nutmeg</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Sweet Filling (Slivková)</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>200 g</strong> Thick traditional plum jam / prune butter (hustý slivkový lekvár) mixed with <strong>1 pinch</strong> ground cinnamon and <strong>10 ml</strong> dark rum</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Toppings & Finishing</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>150 g</strong> Smoked slab bacon (údená slanina), diced into 6 mm lardons (for savory)</li>
    <li><strong>2</strong> Medium yellow onions, sliced and slowly caramelized in bacon fat or butter</li>
    <li><strong>200 g</strong> Full-fat sour cream (kyslá smotana, min. 20% fat)</li>
    <li><strong>60 g</strong> Unsalted butter, melted until foaming (for sweet or combo plating)</li>
    <li><strong>4 L</strong> Water + <strong>15 g</strong> Fine sea salt (for boiling)</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="print-page page-two">
  <div class="print-content-block">
    <h2>Method</h2>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Prep Fillings:</strong> Ensure all fillings are completely chilled and firm before rolling dough. Shape plum jam and potato/bryndza fillings into small compact spoonfuls or balls (~10–12 g each) to make assembling quick.</li>
      <li><strong>Make Dough:</strong> On a clean work surface, combine cold finely grated potatoes, egg, sea salt, and AP flour. Knead quickly with your hands for 2–3 minutes until a smooth, non-sticky, pliable dough forms. Do not overwork.</li>
      <li><strong>Roll & Cut:</strong> Lightly dust the counter with flour. Roll out dough to an even thickness of 2 mm. Cut rounds using an 8 cm cookie cutter or wide glass.</li>
      <li><strong>Fill & Crimp:</strong> Place a ball of filling in the center of each round. Fold the dough over into a half-moon. Firmly pinch the edges together with your fingertips to create an airtight seal, then crimp the border with the tines of a fork.</li>
      <li><strong>Boil Pirohy:</strong> Bring 4 L of salted water to a gentle rolling boil. Drop pirohy in batches, gently stirring from the bottom with a wooden spoon so they don't stick. Once they float to the surface, boil for 2.5–3 minutes until tender.</li>
      <li><strong>Drain & Dress:</strong> Lift pirohy out with a spider strainer directly into a wide bowl. Toss immediately in melted butter or warm rendered bacon fat to prevent sticking.</li>
      <li><strong>Plate the Sweet-and-Sour Combo:</strong> Plate hot savory potato pirohy side-by-side with sweet slivkové pirohy. Top generously with golden caramelized onions, a hearty dollop of cold sour cream, and crispy bacon lardons or warm foaming butter.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-at-bottom">
    <blockquote>
      <strong>Chef's Tip:</strong> This dish has a deep connection to my soul. In our house, my dad would always make pirohy because he learned the craft directly from my grandma. My absolute favorite way to eat them is serving the savory potato and sweet plum jam pirohy together on the same plate, crowned with cold sour cream and slow-caramelized onions—the balance of sweet, savory, tangy, and rich is unmatched. These freeze raw on parchment-lined trays; drop them straight into boiling water frozen.
    </blockquote>
