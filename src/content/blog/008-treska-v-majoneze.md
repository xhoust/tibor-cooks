### Mise en Place (Metric)

**Court-Bouillon & Poached Base:**
- 400 g Skinless Pacific/Atlantic cod fillets (fresh or thawed)
- 120 g Whole carrots (peeled)
- 600 ml Water
- 40 ml White vinegar (5%)
- 1 Bay leaf, 4 black peppercorns, 2 allspice berries, 5 g salt

**Salad Dressing & Vegetables:**
- 60 g Yellow onion (extremely fine brunoise, rinsed in cold water)
- 50 g Dill pickles / gherkins (finely grated and squeezed dry)
- 160 g Good-quality mayonnaise
- 15 g Yellow or Dijon mustard
- 10 ml Fresh lemon juice
- 5 g Granulated sugar
- Fine sea salt & ground white pepper (to taste)

---

### Method & Service Parameters

1. **Court-Bouillon:** In a saucepan, bring water, vinegar, bay leaf, peppercorns, allspice, salt, and peeled whole carrots to a simmer. Cook carrots for 10–12 minutes until tender but holding structure.
2. **Poach Cod:** Add cod fillets into the simmering court-bouillon with the carrots, cover, and gently poach for 7–8 minutes until the fish is opaque and fully cooked.
3. **Chill:** Remove cod and carrots from the broth; cool completely on a wire rack in the refrigerator.
4. **Prep Fish & Vegetables:** Once chilled, finely grate the poached carrots on a box grater. Flake the cold cod into fine, stringy shreds using a fork or gloved hands.
5. **Dressing:** In a mixing bowl, whisk mayonnaise, mustard, lemon juice, sugar, salt, and ground white pepper until smooth and emulsified.
6. **Combine:** Fold flaked cod, grated carrots, rinsed onion brunoise, and squeezed grated pickles into the mayonnaise until uniformly coated.
7. **Mature & Service:** Cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours (ideally overnight) to mature flavors. Serve cold with fresh crusty bread or rožky.
