<div class="print-page">
  <h2>Ingredients</h2>
  
  <h3>Cheese & Assembly (Choose Option)</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Option A (Classic Vegetarian):</strong> <strong>500 g</strong> Young unsmoked Provolone (cut into 4 equal slabs, ~1.5 cm thick, ~125 g each)</li>
    <li><strong>Option B (Layered with Ham):</strong> <strong>16 slices</strong> Young deli provolone + <strong>12 slices</strong> Wafer-thin cooked ham (stacked into 4 portions: 4 layers of cheese alternating with 3 layers of ham)</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Double Breading (Dvojitý Trojobal) & Frying</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>90 g</strong> All-Purpose (AP) flour (unbleached, standard US)</li>
    <li><strong>3</strong> Large eggs, beaten with <strong>30 ml</strong> whole milk and a pinch of salt</li>
    <li><strong>160 g</strong> Fine dry breadcrumbs (strúhanka)</li>
    <li><strong>400 ml–500 ml</strong> Neutral high-heat oil or rendered pork lard (for shallow frying)</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Recommended Side Pairings</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><a href="/blog/022-tatarska-omacka"><strong>Domáca Tatárska Omáčka:</strong></a> Homemade cold tartar sauce with shallots, capers, gherkins, and fresh parsley.</li>
    <li>Boiled baby potatoes tossed in butter and parsley, or crispy fries.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="print-page page-two">
  <div class="print-content-block">
    <h2>Method</h2>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Prep Portions:</strong> 
        <ul>
          <li><em>If making classic slabs:</em> Cut chilled block provolone into four 1.5 cm thick slabs (~125 g each). Pat dry with a paper towel.</li>
          <li><em>If making layered stacks:</em> Assemble 4 individual stacks by alternating 4 slices of deli provolone with 3 slices of wafer-thin ham (Cheese - Ham - Cheese - Ham - Cheese - Ham - Cheese). Press firmly with your palm to compress the layers.</li>
        </ul>
      </li>
      <li><strong>Set Up Breading Station:</strong> Arrange three wide shallow bowls in order: flour in the first, beaten eggs with milk in the second, and dry breadcrumbs in the third.</li>
      <li><strong>Double Bread (Dvojitý Trojobal):</strong>
        <ul>
          <li>Dredge a cheese portion in flour, tapping off excess.</li>
          <li>Dip thoroughly into beaten egg wash.</li>
          <li>Coat lightly in breadcrumbs.</li>
          <li><strong>Second pass:</strong> Dip the breaded portion back into egg wash ensuring no dry spots remain, then press into breadcrumbs a second time, carefully crimping and sealing all four edges and corners.</li>
        </ul>
      </li>
      <li><strong>Freeze:</strong> Transfer double-breaded portions to a parchment-lined tray and freeze for 15–20 minutes to firm up the structural crust.</li>
      <li><strong>Fry:</strong> Heat oil or lard in a heavy skillet over medium-high heat to 175°C–180°C (at least 2 cm deep). Fry cold cheese portions for 90–120 seconds per side until deep golden and blistered. Do not overcrowd the pan.</li>
      <li><strong>Drain & Serve:</strong> Lift out with a slotted spatula onto a wire rack for 30 seconds to drain excess fat. Serve piping hot with a generous side of cold <a href="/blog/022-tatarska-omacka">Domáca Tatárska Omáčka</a> and boiled baby potatoes or fries.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-at-bottom">
    <blockquote>
      <strong>Chef's Tip:</strong> Double-breading and flash-freezing before frying guarantees an airtight seal that prevents cheese blowouts in hot oil. You can also freeze these fully breaded and fry them straight from the freezer whenever needed.
    </blockquote>
