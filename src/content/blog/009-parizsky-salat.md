### Mise en Place (Metric)

- 300 g High-quality cooked ham (cut into 2mm julienne)
- 100 g Dill pickles / gherkins (*kyslé uhorky*, cut into fine julienne)
- 80 g Canned or blanched sweet green peas (drained thoroughly)
- 60 g Yellow onion (extremely fine brunoise, rinsed in cold water and drained)
- 150 g Good-quality mayonnaise (Hellmann's/Duke's or homemade)
- 15 g Yellow mustard (or classic Slovak/Dijon *plnotučná horčica*)
- 10 ml Pickle brine (from the jar)
- 5 ml Worcestershire sauce
- 4 g Granulated sugar
- Fine sea salt & freshly ground black pepper (to taste)

---

### Method & Service Parameters

1. **Cut Meats & Pickles:** Slice the ham into neat, uniform 2mm x 40mm matchsticks (julienne). Cut the dill pickles into matching fine julienne.
2. **Whisk Dressing:** In a mixing bowl, whisk together mayonnaise, mustard, pickle brine, Worcestershire sauce, sugar, salt, and black pepper until smooth and emulsified.
3. **Combine:** Add the julienned ham, pickled cucumber, rinsed and squeezed dry onion brunoise, and well-drained sweet peas to the dressing.
4. **Fold:** Fold everything together gently using a silicone spatula until all ingredients are evenly coated without breaking the peas.
5. **Balance:** Taste and adjust seasoning with a touch more pickle brine for acidity or sugar to balance.
6. **Chill & Cure:** Cover tightly and refrigerate for at least 2 hours (ideally overnight) to mature flavors. Serve chilled with fresh crusty bread (*rožky* or rye loaf).
