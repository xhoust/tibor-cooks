<div class="print-page">
  <h2>Ingredients</h2>
  
  <h3>The Spread Base</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>2 tins (approx. 240 g total / 180 g drained)</strong> Whole sardines in oil, drained</li>
    <li><strong>90 g</strong> Unsalted butter, softened to room temperature</li>
    <li><strong>35 g</strong> Quality mayonnaise</li>
    <li><strong>1/2</strong> Medium white onion, very finely minced</li>
    <li><strong>2 tbsp</strong> Fresh chives, finely snipped</li>
    <li><strong>1 tsp</strong> Dijon or mild yellow mustard</li>
    <li><strong>1–2 tsp</strong> Freshly squeezed lemon juice</li>
    <li><strong>3 g</strong> Fine sea salt</li>
    <li><strong>1 g</strong> Freshly cracked black pepper</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>To Serve</h3>
  <ul>
    <li>Fresh crusty bread or sliced <a href="/blog/011-tradicne-rozky/">Tradičné Rožky</a>.</li>
    <li>Extra salted finely diced white onion and snipped chives on top.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="print-page page-two">
  <div class="print-content-block">
    <h2>Method</h2>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Prep the Onion:</strong> Finely mince the white onion, place it in a small bowl, sprinkle with a pinch of sea salt, and let it sit for 5 minutes. (Salting softens the raw sulfur bite while retaining crisp texture and sweet onion flavor).</li>
      <li><strong>Whip the Base:</strong> In a mixing bowl, combine the softened butter and mayonnaise. Whip with a fork or spatula until pale, airy, and creamy.</li>
      <li><strong>Incorporate the Sardines:</strong> Add the drained sardines. Mash thoroughly with a fork, breaking down the whole fish and incorporating the tender bones directly into the butter base until evenly textured.</li>
      <li><strong>Fold Aromatics & Season:</strong> Fold in the salted minced white onion, fresh snipped chives, mustard, lemon juice, remaining sea salt, and freshly cracked black pepper. Stir until fully cohesive.</li>
      <li><strong>Taste & Serve:</strong> Taste and adjust with extra lemon juice or black pepper. Slather generously on thick slices of fresh bread or halved rožky, finishing with an extra pinch of salted white onion and chives over the top.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-at-bottom">
    <blockquote>
      <strong>Chef's Tip:</strong> Whole sardines in oil are unmatched for depth and flavor. Salting the finely diced white onion before folding it in alongside fresh chives cuts through the richness of the butter and oily fish without overpowering the spread with raw heat.
    </blockquote>
