### Mise en Place (Metric)

- 800 g Russet potatoes (boiled in skins, chilled, and finely grated)
- 200–240 g All-Purpose (AP) flour
- 6 g Fine sea salt
- 80 g Melted duck fat, lard, or clarified butter

---

### Method & Service Parameters

1. **Form Dough:** Work cold grated potatoes, salt, and AP flour quickly by hand into a smooth, supple dough.
2. **Portion & Roll:** Divide into 10–12 balls (~80g each). Roll thin (~2mm) on a floured surface.
3. **Dry Griddle:** Cook on a dry cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat for 1–2 minutes per side until blistered.
4. **Brush & Stack:** Transfer to a plate and brush immediately with hot duck fat. Stack under a clean towel.
